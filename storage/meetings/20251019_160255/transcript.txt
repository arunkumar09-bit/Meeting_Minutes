The cultural, Sci-Tech, and meeting rooms in SNCC are ready but cannot be used because there is no furniture. It was mentioned that the furniture budget does not fall under the Gymkhana budget, and efforts are being made to secure funds through JICA. Since the JICA funding window closes on 31st March, the preparation of the furniture list needs to be completed urgently. It was decided that by 28th January, a detailed list of all required furniture items for each room, along with estimated costs, should be prepared. The finalized list will then be reviewed in a meeting scheduled for Monday to discuss and confirm the budget and allocation.
